2. Write or paste your notes

One line = one step (one note, or several notes played together). Format: beats | left hand | right hand

🎹 Piano format

1 | C3 | C4left C3, right C4, 1 beat
2 | - | E4no left hand, E4 for 2 beats
1 | C3 E3 G3 | C4 E4 G4chords: separate notes with spaces
0.5 | - | F#4F sharp, half a beat
1 | - | Bb3B flat
1 | - | -rest (silence)
  • Letters: C D E F G A B. # = sharp, b = flat.
  • The number is the octave. C4 = middle C. No number: left hand 3, right hand 4.
  • Beats: 1 = quarter note, 2 = half, 4 = whole, 0.5 = eighth.
  • - = nothing. Lines starting with # are comments.

🎸 Guitar format

Way A: letters, same as piano. Put notes in the right column; the app chooses the frets.

1 | - | E4one note
2 | - | C4 E4 G4notes together (chord)

Way B: tab style, string/fret. Strings: 1 = thinnest (high E) … 6 = thickest (low E). Fret 0 = open string.

1 | - | 1/0string 1, open
1 | - | 2/1string 2, fret 1 (= C)
2 | - | 5/3 4/2 3/0 2/1 1/0open C chord

Open strings (6→1): E A D G B E. The left/right columns only change the color; for guitar just use the right column.
