1. Prepare sheet music

Choose a picture or PDF. Drag across the preview to select a crop.

2. Load recognized notes

This version does not automatically recognize notation from pictures. Use Audiveris locally to recognize the prepared image and export uncompressed MusicXML, then import it here. Check recognition against your score.

Or edit notes manually. One line is one step; format: beats | left hand | right hand. Use spaces for simultaneous notes, a dash for a rest, and octave numbers (C4 = middle C).
